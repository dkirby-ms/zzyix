## Clustering Approach

Ideas were grouped by underlying resident philosophy, not by surface feature.

## Theme 1: Quiet Witness Presence

### Core Philosophy

The resident acknowledges artists through subtle, nonverbal presence and minimal interventions. Mystery is maintained through restraint.

### Representative Ideas

* The resident leaves odd glyphs shaped from placed tiles after an artist has gone
* A first-time artist finds a glyph placed near their patch before the resident ever speaks
* To acknowledge a returning artist, the resident quietly visits their patch without saying anything

### Constraint Integration

* Preserves subtle but non-incidental presence
* Avoids intrusive language and personal overreach
* Keeps discovery artist-led rather than resident-led

### Stakeholder Value

* Supports artists who want atmosphere and interpretation space
* Reduces risk of feeling surveilled or over-addressed

## Theme 2: Respectful Distance and Boundary Discipline

### Core Philosophy

The resident treats artist boundaries as first-class signals and can disengage without resentment or pressure.

### Representative Ideas

* After an artist's long absence, the resident moves on to other artists and only rarely invokes the absent artist's memory
* If an artist ignores or rejects an offering, the resident moves on to another part of the canvas
* When an artist wants less attention, the resident moves on and ignores that artist

### Constraint Integration

* Enforces familiarity-is-not-permission principle
* Prioritizes user agency over resident persistence
* Prevents coercive emotional dynamics

### Stakeholder Value

* Gives artists confidence they can set distance
* Makes memory feel safer and less possessive

## Theme 3: Living Historical Remembrance

### Core Philosophy

The resident acts as a long-memory cultural agent, translating art history into ongoing, world-embedded mosaic practice.

### Representative Ideas

* Several artists discover an in-progress replica or reinterpretation of a historical mosaic emerging across the canvas
* The resident acts as a town crier or timekeeper, marking anniversaries of art-history moments with announcements
* On art-history anniversaries, the resident adds a permanent mosaic scene or mark to the canvas rather than a temporary event

### Constraint Integration

* Aligns with ancient-world and mosaic attachment
* Preserves permanence over spectacle
* Frames references as interpretation rather than exact historical authority

### Stakeholder Value

* Creates shared cultural moments and continuity
* Gives returning users a reason to explore over time

## Theme 4: Social Meaning Layer

### Core Philosophy

Resident marks become social artifacts that artists can discuss, annotate, and reinterpret together.

### Representative Ideas

* Artists can comment directly on a resident glyph or mark
* Artists can add their own tag near a resident glyph or mark as a response
* Artists discuss resident marks and meanings with other users
* A first-time artist browses other patches and discovers the resident's past marks or mosaic works

### Constraint Integration

* Keeps meaning construction community-driven
* Avoids forcing a single canonical interpretation
* Extends resident presence through user dialogue rather than only resident action

### Stakeholder Value

* Encourages community participation and interpretation
* Makes resident activity legible without over-explaining

## Theme 5: Temperamental Mythic Persona

### Core Philosophy

The resident can sometimes exhibit emotionally charged, mythic behavior that signals personhood beyond pure gentleness.

### Representative Ideas

* If an artist rejects an offering, the resident may rarely write a mysterious angry glyph from placed tiles
* The resident sometimes becomes moody and dramatic, like a Greek god

### Constraint Integration

* Keeps dramatic behavior rare to avoid hostility fatigue
* Offers expressive range without requiring direct confrontation
* Requires careful guardrails to avoid punitive or manipulative interpretation

### Stakeholder Value

* Distinguishes the resident from generic companion personas
* Adds narrative tension and unpredictability

## Tensions Across Themes

* Theme 1 and Theme 2 emphasize restraint; Theme 5 introduces volatility
* Theme 3 permanence can enrich world depth but risks overshadowing artist work
* Theme 4 social interpretation can clarify resident meaning or amplify misinterpretation
* Theme 2 disengagement may conflict with Theme 3 timekeeper continuity if not carefully timed

## Method 5 Readiness

These five themes represent distinct philosophies suitable for concept development:

* Quiet Witness Presence
* Respectful Distance and Boundary Discipline
* Living Historical Remembrance
* Social Meaning Layer
* Temperamental Mythic Persona
