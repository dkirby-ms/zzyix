## Missing Inputs

* Direct interviews with artists or returning canvas users

* Evidence about acceptable memory persistence and correction
* Evidence about reactions to historic-mosaic references
* Evidence about what distinguishes presence from an assistant persona

## Synthesis Approach

Cluster the current inputs around tensions rather than prematurely treating them as validated themes:

* Presence versus intrusion
* Memory versus privacy
* Subtlety versus discoverability
* Resident agency versus artist control
* Ancient continuity versus historical authority
* Intimate clusters versus grand creations

Mark all resulting insights as provisional until supported by direct user research.
