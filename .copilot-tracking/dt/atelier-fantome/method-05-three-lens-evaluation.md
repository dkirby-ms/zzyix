## Evaluation Mode

This is a rapid in-session evaluation using simulated stakeholder perspectives from the product-owner and chief-developer viewpoint. External validation is still required.

## Stakeholder Perspectives Simulated

* First-time artist
* Returning artist
* Boundary-sensitive artist who wants less attention
* Community participant interested in shared interpretation
* Product and engineering owner balancing complexity and risk

## Concept Scores

Scale: 1 (low) to 5 (high)

| Concept | Desirability | Feasibility | Viability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet Witness | 5 | 4 | 5 | Strong identity fit, low intrusion risk, clear baseline behavior |
| Respectful Distance | 5 | 5 | 5 | High trust value and straightforward behavior rule |
| Social Meaning | 4 | 4 | 4 | Strong community upside, moderate moderation and UX complexity |
| Historic Echoes | 4 | 2 | 3 | Powerful world depth, high implementation and governance cost |
| Mythic Temperament | 3 | 3 | 3 | Distinctive flavor but risk of hostile interpretation if mis-tuned |

## Lens Highlights

### Desirability

* Quiet Witness and Respectful Distance best preserve intrigue without surveillance pressure
* Social Meaning increases engagement by letting artists co-interpret resident marks
* Historic Echoes is attractive as rare events, not constant behavior
* Mythic Temperament is polarizing and should remain optional and sparse

### Feasibility

* Quiet Witness and Respectful Distance are easiest to prototype in lo-fi form
* Social Meaning is feasible with basic comment and tag interaction assumptions
* Historic Echoes likely needs stronger content governance and event timing logic
* Mythic Temperament requires careful guardrails and language constraints

### Viability

* Trust-preserving concepts score highest for long-term retention and community health
* Social Meaning is viable when interpretation noise is controlled
* Historic and Mythic spikes are viable as low-frequency differentiators, not core loop

## Method 6 Prototype Recommendation

Prioritize these first two concepts for low-fidelity prototyping:

1. Quiet Witness
2. Social Meaning

Include Respectful Distance as a non-negotiable guardrail behavior across both prototypes.

## Selection Rationale

* Quiet Witness captures the core resident identity and mystery model
* Social Meaning tests whether resident marks can become community artifacts
* Respectful Distance prevents prototypes from drifting into intrusive behavior
* Historic Echoes and Mythic Temperament stay in reserve as spike behaviors after baseline trust is validated

## Method 6 Test Focus

* Do artists notice and interpret quiet resident signals?
* Do artists feel intrigued rather than watched?
* Do comments and tags around resident marks improve engagement or create confusion?
* Do boundary signals reliably reduce resident attention when users want distance?
