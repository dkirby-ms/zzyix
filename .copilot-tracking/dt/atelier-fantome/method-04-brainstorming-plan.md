## Challenge

Generate ways for the resident to feel alive, mysterious, and artistically active in the shared canvas without becoming intrusive, controlling, or incidental.

## Constraints

* Artist work is never overwritten or claimed by the resident
* Creative history may inform offerings; sensitive conversation memory remains private
* Presence should provoke curiosity without demanding attention
* Grand creations are rare and interpret historic mosaics rather than reproducing them
* The resident is a distinct inhabitant, not a command-driven tool

## Divergent Target

Generate at least 15 rough ideas spanning at least five different philosophies before evaluation or selection.

## Perspectives

* First-time artist seeking orientation and intrigue
* Returning artist noticing continuity
* Artist who wants distance or rejects an offering
* Resident preserving a long-lived mosaic world
* Shared-canvas community encountering a rare grand creation

## Idea Categories

* Quiet visual echoes
* Recurring patterns and seasonal change
* Invitations and discovery trails
* Responses to absence, return, acceptance, or rejection
* Historic-mosaic remembrance
* Community-scale events
