## Evaluation Setup

Stakeholder simulation mode was used in-session with the product owner and chief developer perspective. External stakeholder validation is still required.

## Concept Purpose And Validation Targets

### Quiet Witness

Purpose:

* Establish the resident's default identity as subtle but perceivable presence

Validation Target:

* Artists report intrigue and discovery without feeling monitored

### Respectful Distance

Purpose:

* Prove boundary-respecting disengagement when artists want less attention

Validation Target:

* Artists feel in control of attention and resident proximity

### Social Meaning

Purpose:

* Test whether resident marks can trigger healthy community interpretation

Validation Target:

* Artists use comments, tags, and discussion to co-create meaning without confusion overload

### Historic Echoes

Purpose:

* Test whether rare permanent historical references increase world depth

Validation Target:

* Artists experience awe and continuity without perceiving resident takeover of the canvas

### Mythic Temperament

Purpose:

* Add expressive emotional range while preserving trust and non-punitive behavior

Validation Target:

* Artists interpret dramatic moments as character depth, not hostility or manipulation

## Risks To Watch

* Quiet witness may become too subtle and read as incidental
* Social meaning may increase interpretive noise and confusion
* Historical permanence may overshadow artist-created work
* Mythic temperament may feel unsafe if frequency or tone is uncontrolled

## Next Evaluation Step

Run low-fidelity concept walkthroughs and capture D/F/V reactions for each concept before selecting Method 6 prototype priorities.
